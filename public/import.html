1. Prepare your file

Download template

Save your sheet as CSV (in Excel: File, Save As, “CSV UTF-8”). It needs a header row with Name, Phone and Plan. End date is optional: when it is empty, the plan’s length counted from today is used.

Dates can be written 2026-12-31 or 31/12/2026. Plan names must match your plans (any capital letters). Members with the same name and phone number are skipped.
